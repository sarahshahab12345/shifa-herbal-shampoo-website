A kitchen recipe, not a factory formula

Shifa isn't made on a production line. Every bottle starts the same way it always has — herbs soaked overnight, simmered slowly, then strained and blended by hand in a home kitchen.

Batches are cooked to keep up with orders, not stocked in a warehouse. If a size runs out, it usually means a fresh pot is already on the stove.

No sulphates, no parabens, no shortcuts — just the ingredients your grandmother's shampoo probably had, updated for a wash-and-go routine.

From the pot to the bottle

Three steps, no shortcuts — the same way every batch has been made.

1

Soak & simmer

Reetha, shikakai and amla soak overnight, then simmer slowly until the herbs release everything they've got.

2

Hand-blend

The cooled extract is strained and blended by hand with aloe vera and essential oils.

3

Bottle by bottle

Every bottle is filled, capped and labelled one at a time, the same day it's made.
